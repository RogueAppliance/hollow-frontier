# Assets

Every texture and GeckoLib model the mod uses. Paths are relative to `src/main/resources/assets/hollowfrontier/`.
Placeholders come from `.\gradlew.bat genPlaceholders` (flat 16x16 color + 1px border) and get swapped for real art later.
Third-party art is only allowed under a license that permits bundling (CC0 / CC-BY); record the source and license here.
The optional compat mods (JEI, Jade, Curios) are compiled against but never bundled; nothing of theirs is in our jar.

**Release pass (phase 14, 2026-10-29):** every file under `textures/`, `geckolib/` and `sounds/` is listed below, and every path listed exists. Textures no model or code uses are taken out of the jar.

## Art tools

- `tools/textures/TextureArt.java` draws every current texture as "pixel art v1":
  - **Item sprites:** ASCII grids where digits 1–6 are shades of a base color and letters are accents.
  - **Blocks:** procedural tiles (stone and deepslate noise, ore clusters, riveted metal, planks, bricks).
  - **Skins and armor:** box-UV painters.

  Regenerate with `java tools/textures/TextureArt.java src/main/resources/assets/hollowfrontier/textures`. New content gets `genPlaceholders` placeholders until it's added there.
- `tools/models/GunModels.java` builds the gun geo models from code (one blueprint per gun):
  - **Space:** grip at the origin, barrel toward -Z, +Y up. GeckoLib mirrors X, so the gun's right side (lock, hammer) is negative x.
  - **Faces:** every face is UV-mapped onto a material swatch of the 128x64 gun texture at 2 texels per pixel (wood, steel, case-hardened, brass, bright steel, black iron, hickory, bore, chambers, copper). The swatch layout lives in both `GunModels.Mat` and `TextureArt.Gun`; keep them in sync.
  - **Animations:** the reload loop length must equal the gun's `GunStats.loadTicks` (one chamber per loop).

  Regenerate with `java tools/models/GunModels.java src/main/resources/assets/hollowfrontier/geckolib/models/item`.
- `tools/models/AirshipModels.java` builds the airship's geo model the same way: feet at the origin, bow toward -Z, 16 px to a block; every face maps onto a swatch of the 128x64 airship atlas (`AirshipModels.Mat` and `TextureArt.Expedition.atlas` share the layout), stretched at most 1 texel per pixel. Regenerate with `java tools/models/AirshipModels.java src/main/resources/assets/hollowfrontier/geckolib/models/entity`.
- `tools/music/PianoRoll.java` renders the saloon piano's tunes from MIDI scores (`tools/music/scores/`) with its own additive piano synthesizer: no samples, so nothing third-party is in the sound. Each key has 1-3 strings with inharmonic partials, tuned a few cents apart and a little off pitch (an old upright, slightly honky-tonk), a felt-hammer thump, dampers on key release, a little humanised timing and velocity, then a small-room reverb. Render, then convert like the other music:
  - `java tools/music/PianoRoll.java tools/music/scores/<tune>.mid <tune>.wav`
  - ffmpeg two-pass `loudnorm` (I=-18, TP=-1.5, LRA=11, linear), `-ac 1 -ar 44100 -c:a libvorbis -q:a 4 -map_metadata -1 -fflags +bitexact`.
- `tools/music/Swell.java` renders the Hollow Earth's reveal (`java tools/music/Swell.java reveal.wav`, then the same ffmpeg settings): an 11-second additive-synthesis swell of soft string-and-choir voices on an open E major chord with the ninth, over a low drone, with a trembling high shimmer, a crescendo to 5 s and a long fade, in a Schroeder hall reverb. No samples. Checked by spectrogram and waveform only, not by ear.
- `tools/music/Laser.java` renders the phlogiston guns' shots (`java tools/music/Laser.java build/laser`, then each `ffmpeg -i <shot>.wav -ac 1 -ar 44100 -c:a libvorbis -q:a 4 -map_metadata -1 -fflags +bitexact <shot>.ogg`; no loudness pass, as they're peak-normalised to -1 dBFS in the synth).
- Visual check: `.\gradlew.bat runPhoto` opens a client on `run/saves/hf_photo` (copy any world there first). It saves `run/screenshots/hf_*.png`, then quits. Shots:
  - the humanoids from the front, close-up, side and back;
  - the player with the revolver in third and first person;
  - the block rows (decor, materials, a lit steam line);
  - an item-frame wall with every mod item;
  - an armor stand in the cowboy set;
  - the worn Cowboy Hat on armor stands (front, three-quarter, side, head tilted), a zombie and the player (`hf_h0`-`hf_h3`; `-PphotoScene=hat` alone);
  - the railroad depot with its standpipe, a parked handcar and one pulling away with the engineer aboard, the handcar in hand, and both advancement tabs (`hf_35`-`hf_40`);
  - each mod creative tab open in the creative inventory (`hf_41`-`hf_43`);
  - the expedition: a Polar Gateway crater dark, waking, its beam from afar and open, the airship parked (side, three-quarter, bow, above) and under way, at the helm in first and third person, the Polar Compass and Resonator in hand, the Steam tab, and a crossing into the Hollow Earth with its return gateway and the view (`hf_44`-`hf_58`). `gradlew runPhoto -PphotoScene=expedition` shoots just this scene.
  - the Hollow Earth (`hf_60`-`hf_77`; `-PphotoScene=hollow` alone): the crossing as a player makes it (down Symmes' shaft; out of the crust looking down, up at the inner sun, and out over the land mid-fall; landed), the sun and the crust from the ground and the horizon, then each biome from high above and from its ground.

## Sounds

The mod's sound events (`ModSounds`) are defined in `sounds.json` by `ModSoundDefinitionsProvider`. Until real recordings exist, each one is a remix of vanilla sound events (type `event`, re-pitched and mixed); give an entry real `.ogg` files to re-voice it without touching code. Music is real recordings (below), converted with ffmpeg to Ogg Vorbis 44.1 kHz q4, loudness-normalised (EBU R128, -18 LUFS) and stripped of metadata; all are mono, since jukeboxes and the piano play them from a position and only mono sounds fade with distance.
